# AI Transcription — video & audio to subtitles

> Transcribe video and audio to SRT and VTT subtitles in your browser with AI speech-to-text. Audio is extracted locally — only the audio track is uploaded.

- Start a job in the web app: https://ai.opensubtitles.com/new (opensubtitles.com account required)
- Output formats: SRT or VTT
- Pricing is per second of audio in credits: see [pricing](https://ai.opensubtitles.com/pricing.md)
- API: see the [API reference](https://ai.opensubtitles.com/api-docs.md) (`POST /ai/transcribe`)

## How transcription works

1. **Add your file** — Upload a video or audio file in your browser. No installation required.
2. **Transcribe with AI speech-to-text** — Your file is converted to audio locally and only the audio track is uploaded for transcription.
3. **Download your subtitles** — Get your transcript as SRT or VTT subtitle files, ready to use.

## What you get

- **Subtitle formats:** Download SRT and VTT subtitle files for players and editors.
- **Video and audio support:** Transcribe common video and audio files, such as MP4, MKV, MOV, MP3, WAV, and M4A.
- **Privacy-conscious processing:** Media is converted locally in your browser; only the extracted audio is sent for transcription, not your full video file.
- **Transcribe, then translate:** Create subtitles in the spoken language first, then translate them into another language as a separate step.

## FAQ

### What happens to my file?

Media is converted to a mono MP3 in your browser; only that audio track is uploaded for transcription.

### Which formats work?

Common video and audio formats are supported. You always receive SRT or VTT subtitles.

### What if the transcription is in the wrong language?

The selected language must match the spoken language of your file. Transcription and translation are separate steps — transcribe first, then translate the result into any language.

### Do I need to install anything?

No. Transcription runs in your browser — no installation required.

### Can I translate my subtitles?

Yes. After transcribing, you can translate the resulting subtitles into another language. Translation is a separate step.

### How accurate is the transcription?

Quality and accuracy depend on factors that are hard to determine in advance. More capable (and usually more expensive) models often produce better results, but background noise and overall audio quality also play a major role. For best results, use clear audio and make sure the selected language matches the spoken language.
